New Taipei district map

Geometry: taiwan-atlas 2021.9.20, towns-10t.json
https://github.com/dkaoster/taiwan-atlas
https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/taiwan-atlas@2021.9.20/towns-10t.json
Derived from Taiwan Ministry of the Interior township boundaries:
https://data.gov.tw/dataset/7441

The dashboard retains the 29 New Taipei district geometries, with Taipei City
and Keelung City county geometry as excluded neighboring context, and projects
them to SVG coordinates. Boundaries are a simplified 2021 atlas edition; population
figures come from the selected year's cleaned CSV, not from the atlas.
